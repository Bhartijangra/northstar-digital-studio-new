What is SEO?

SEO (search engine optimisation) is the practice of helping your pages appear in unpaid search results for relevant searches.

How search engines work

They crawl pages, index them, then rank results by relevance and quality. Learn more in technical SEO explained.

Types of SEO

On-page covers content and HTML. Technical covers crawling and speed. Off-page covers links and mentions. Local covers nearby searches.

How to start

Research keywords, give each page one clear topic, write helpful content and track results. Our SEO services page shows the full process.

Common beginner mistake

Targeting the same keyword on many pages makes them compete.

FAQs

Is SEO free?

Clicks are free, but time and expertise cost money.

How long does it take?

Usually months, depending on competition.
