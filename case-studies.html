Educational examples

A: Local fitness studio

Situation: A new studio with few online enquiries.

Problems: Thin service pages, no call tracking, inconsistent listings.

Strategy: Improve local relevance and track enquiries.

Actions: Rewrote service pages, fixed listings, added enquiry tracking.

B: Online fashion store

Situation: A store relying on paid ads.

Problems: Duplicate titles, weak category copy, poor internal links.

Strategy: Strengthen category pages and structure.

Actions: Unique metadata, category content, internal links, helpful guides.
